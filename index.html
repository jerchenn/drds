The session
What today is for
How to approach empirical research.
Use abstract statistical theory, when needed.
Ensure we understand the intuition, always.
Traverse from theoretical foundations to practical applications.
Getting you to think about data, and getting familiar with Stata.
Introducing you to some cool methods, to impress your cohort mates.
The path
Five pages, in order
IBig IdeasCausation, the ladder of causal questions, the causal objective, and the divide between quantitative and qualitative research.Open IIOrchard CensusStatistics: population and sample, sampling strategies, descriptive and inferential statistics, the law of large numbers, and the central limit theorem.Open IIIMonty HallProbability: a game to play, a simulation to run, Bayes' rule, and the step from the host's rule to the identification assumption.Open IVThe Do-FileStata: import, clean, describe, graph, and regress, then from an association to a difference in differences.Open VFive DesignsRandomised trials, difference in differences, synthetic control, regression discontinuity, and instrumental variables, through the studies the lecture uses.Open
